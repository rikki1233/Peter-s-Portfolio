Project Overview

This project focused on creating a concept banking app for Nigerian Gen Z that reimagines how young people interact with money. The goal was to design a modern, relatable, and secure digital banking experience that resonates with their lifestyle while addressing the limitations of existing solutions.

The Challenge

Current banking apps in Nigeria often feel outdated, complicated, and unrelatable to Gen Z users. Pain points include:

  • Overly corporate design that doesn't appeal to younger audiences.
  • Confusing navigation and cluttered interfaces.
  • Lack of engaging financial tools tailored to young people.

Gen Z wants speed, simplicity, and personalization—and existing apps are failing to deliver that.

The Solution

A secure, playful, and social banking app designed to make money management simple and engaging for Nigerian Gen Z.

Impact & Results

Since this was a conceptual project, no real-world data exists yet. However, usability feedback showed:

Users found the app more approachable and fun compared to existing banks

Most testers found key flows (onboarding, transfer, savings) faster than with their current apps.

Strong interest in gamified savings features, which encouraged consistent use.

Key Learnings

  • Gen Z values simplicity and relatability as much as functionality
  • Gamification can make financial discipline more engaging
  • Biometric authentication significantly improves user experience
  • Nigerian cultural elements (like "ajo") can be digitized successfully to drive adoption.
