Project Overview

This project explored the design of an e-commerce platform for my streetwear brand, with a unique twist: a built-in thrift marketplace where fans can resell authentic pieces. The goal was to create not only a sleek online store but also a community-driven ecosystem that extends the life and value of each product.

The Challenge

Streetwear thrives on drops, scarcity, and resale value. Traditional online stores only focus on selling new items, missing out on the culture of reselling and community interaction. This gap creates:

  • Missed opportunities for brand engagement.
  • Fragmented resale happening on external platforms.
  • Lost chances to reinforce exclusivity and hype.

The Solution

The new design features a clean, card-based interface with biometric authentication, simplified navigation, and real-time security indicators. We implemented a progressive disclosure pattern to reduce cognitive load while maintaining access to advanced features.

Impact & Results

Community-driven resale that lets buyers also sell, boosting engagement and loyalty

Sustainable fashion approach by extending product life through thrifts.

Simplified checkout flows that reduce friction for both buyers and sellers.

Increased brand value by creating hype around resale and exclusivity.

Key Learnings

  • Simplicity in flows (checkout, seller onboarding) is critical to adoption.
  • Visual hierarchy should always prioritize the product in fashion design.
  • Trust signals (ratings, reviews, escrow payments) are essential in resale marketplaces.
  • Clear visual hierarchy reduces user errors
